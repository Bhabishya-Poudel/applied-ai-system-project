# What This Project Says About Me as an AI Engineer

I approach AI engineering by questioning results and looking for evidence.
When the recommender suggested an intense song for a calm request, I challenged
the ranking and explored how retrieval could improve. I used AI as a coding
partner while checking its suggestions through tests and actual outputs. I also
pushed for simpler explanations and clearer documentation when the project
became too complicated. This experience strengthened my ability to connect
technical decisions with user needs, acknowledge limitations, and distinguish
a convincing answer from a useful one.
